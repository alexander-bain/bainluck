## RULING 4 — TTL 65 s is RATIFIED and TTL derivation is CLOSED. The period is the finding.
addressee: program:latency

65 s is ratified. It is derived, not chosen: 53.920 s quantises to a 60 s period, so
59/60 leave zero headroom, and 65 is the first value the live beat has ever graded SAFE
against the module's own `SAFETY_MARGIN_S`. Measurement over argument — ship it.

**Do not spend another cycle deriving a TTL.** The lane's own numbers say the TTL buys
5.7 points, not a third, because 196–547 s stalls lose all 40 regardless of TTL; zeroing
that loss needs TTL ≥ 553 s, which is a staleness answer nobody wants. A TTL large
enough to survive the regressed period is a decision to serve stale data instead of
fixing the regression.

The finding is the **period regression**: 42.5–51.7 s → 40.1–547.2 s, p50 196.2 s — a
3.8–10.6× regression, mechanism named as #2014's `expires: 10` against a ~47 s pass.
That is the next latency queue's headline. The TTL is a floor under the damage, not the
repair.

Two things carried forward and not dropped:
- The published "buys a third" figure was corrected in public at n=13 after being
  published at n=6. That correction is the behaviour we want; it is recorded as a win,
  not a fault.
- The coverage guard finding stands as doctrine: `enrich_snippet_angles` fell out of the
  stamp arm, so the headline number improved because the instrument went blinder. The
  census caught it. **A metric that improves while its denominator shrinks is a defect
  until proven otherwise** — keep the census wired as a standing guard on every latency
  stamp read.

## STANDING — the wrong-gate mechanism (ruling (c)'s third form)
addressee: all lanes

Q378 earned a third failure mechanism, in a new direction. The first two were about a
write being impossible. This one is **a green gate being the wrong gate**: the Flow
Sentinel check that should have caught #2020 tests `reconciled == 0` while `reconciled`

Related, same class, from the same window: two gitleaks runs scanned different ranges
(PR-range vs push-range) and returned **opposite verdicts on the same commit**. Reading
only the passing one would have shipped a red PR.

Standing rule, effective now: **a green gate is evidence only if you can state what it
would have to see to go red, and that statement matches the defect class you are
claiming it covers.** Any lane citing a green gate as proof names the failing input it
would catch. "It passed" is not a finding.
