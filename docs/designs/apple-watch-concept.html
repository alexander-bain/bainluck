Why Apple Watch?

⏱

Glanceable

Win probability is a number. Numbers belong on watch faces. No scrolling, no loading screens.

💫

Haptic Context

A tap on your wrist when your team surges or an upset unfolds. The only device that can literally nudge you.

🎯

Micro Games

Higher/Lower is a 2-second interaction. Perfect for the elevator, the checkout line, the commercial break.

Technical Approach

WidgetKit

Complications and Smart Stack widgets. TimelineProvider refreshes every 15 min.

WatchConnectivity

Sync followed markets and preferences from the iPhone app.

Live Activities

ActivityKit for in-progress games. Dynamic Island mirrored to Watch.

Push Notifications

Backend sends probability-shift alerts via APNs with actionable categories.

SwiftUI Shared Code

Existing iOS codebase is SwiftUI. Watch shares models, API client, prediction logic.

Status

Prototype deployed to device May 4, 2026. Guess game, live games, glances working.
