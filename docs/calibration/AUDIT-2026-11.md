## 4. Contamination

### 4.1 The 109918 class: settled on the date alone, retraction marker, no venue result

**Definition, in database terms.** A market is in the class when all of the following hold:
- `market_metadata.resolution_gate.reason = 'resolution_date_elapsed_no_venue_result'`;
- `settled_at` is on or after Sep 1;
- at least one leg has `resolution_source = 'ungradeable_result'`;
- no leg has `is_winner = true`.

**Writer.** `mark_resolved_futures` (`backend/app/tasks/futures.py:1276-1433`) runs on beat `crontab(minute=15, hour="2,8,14,20")` (`tasks/__init__.py:9041-9044`). Once `resolution_date` has passed, it writes `status='resolved'` and `settled_at`, unless the linked event is live or still ahead (#8586). It writes no grade on any leg. The `ungradeable_result` marker comes from the #1852 retraction rail (`backend/app/utils/kalshi_fabricated_loss.py:145`), not from this task.

**Count: 87 markets, all Kalshi.**
- 3 are one-leg markets: 109918, 2260300 and 56943616.
- 64 were settled in a single run at 2026-11-03T20:16:37Z (12:16 PM PT), and one more earlier that day at 12:18 AM PT. The 64 are mostly election markets: state legislatures, California propositions, mayors, governors, and `KXIMPEACH`. That run settled 602 markets in total, 497 of them Polymarket.
- 14 were settled on Nov 1 and 8 between Oct 1 and Oct 30.
- The ids are in Appendix A.

**Do they enter the record?** Not the curve. `ungradeable_result` counts as a terminal void and is ineligible at every market shape: evaluated from `resolution_authority.py:354-364, 396-425`, `is_calibration_truth_eligible('ungradeable_result', n_outcomes=1)` returns `False`. The published population applies that test at `precompute_calibration.py:5155`, and the multi-leg markets also fall to the no-winner rule (`4351-4355`). They do enter two counts:
- `total_markets`, which is 2,044,150 and counts every market with `status='resolved'` (`precompute_calibration.py:7925-7935`);
- `truth_evidence.ineligible_other`, which is 77,058 outcomes in 34,113 markets and covers all ineligible sources together.

On the page, these markets read "Result unavailable" (#11947, and #11334 for the same class). When the venue does grade them, band 1 of the Kalshi grader skips legs that carry the marker (`backfill_winners.py:455-462`). Band 2 reaches them later.

**What `calibration_population_integrity.py` says about them.** It has no rule for a date-alone settlement: the evaluated row returns `[]`. Evaluated on a re-open that keeps the marker, the state #11924 sized at 33,172 legs across 4,254 open markets, it returns `reopen_retains_grade`. Its corpus is pinned to q267 (`audited_commit 71571f4a`) and has no scenario for either state.

### 4.2 The adjacent class that does enter: one-leg markets the venue voided (#11953)

- 3,859 of them carry a grade the curve accepts, so unless another rule removes them they are scored as losses: `api_settlement` 3,728, `clean_resolution` 101, `all_losers` 30.
- The 1,101 Kalshi `api_settlement` legs among them are all priced under 5%, which fits a real "No". That is not contamination on this evidence.

- The numeric effect is small. The defect is a void recorded as a result. Ids and the smallest fix are in #11953 and Appendix B.

## Appendix C: how to reproduce

- **Date-alone census.** Run over 2-day `settled_at` slices from Sep 1 to Nov 4, using `ix_futures_markets_settled_at` (33 reads, 0.6–6.4 s each). Filter on `market_metadata->'resolution_gate'->>'reason' = 'resolution_date_elapsed_no_venue_result'`, aggregate the legs per market with `COUNT(...) FILTER (WHERE ...)`, and count markets by `n_legs`, `n_ungradeable`, `n_winners` and the eligible-grade count.
- **Eligibility.** Evaluate `app.utils.resolution_authority.is_calibration_truth_eligible(source, n_outcomes=…)` from the worktree.
- **Forward horizons.** Use `ix_fm_feed_open_timely` (open, non-event, `resolution_date` set) joined to `MAX(futures_odds_snapshots.captured_at)` per outcome, by band and source. The 7–30-day Kalshi band timed out once and was re-read in four sub-bands.
- **Backward horizons.** Nine single-day `settled_at` slices with `fm.id % 20 = 0`; four `LATERAL … ORDER BY captured_at DESC LIMIT 1` probes per outcome at the T-30/T-7/T-1/T-0 cutoffs.
- **Guardrail.** The payload's own `buckets`, folded with the page's `normalizeCat`, then `aggregateBuckets`, then `ece` / `mce`, for the default cohort (`price_moved !== false`) and the all cohort. Also the payload's `by_category` as published.
