What the timestamp actually means, traced to the query: it is MAX(futures_odds_snapshots.captured_at), and every refresh writes a snapshot whether or not the number moved. So it is the last time a probability for that question reached us — not created, not last updated by the venue, not last traded. And it cannot tell you which of two causes it has: the market may be quoted and untraded, or our reader may not be covering it.
