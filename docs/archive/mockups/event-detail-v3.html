Design Notes

Real data from event 12080338 (BOS @ ATL, live right now). 185 related futures across 3 sources.

Level 1 -- Win Probability (Hero)

Hero card is unchanged from today. Kalshi moneyline (56.5% ATL) shown as a source alongside ESPN and BainLuck stat model.

Level 2 -- Total Points Spectrum

Instead of showing "O/U 221.5" (gambling-style), we show a probability spectrum across all the threshold markets Kalshi offers (216.5, 219.5, 222.5, etc). This visualizes WHERE the market thinks the total will land, like a probability distribution. The current pace marker shows what's happening live.

Level 3 -- Player Props

Swim lanes with live progress. Each shows: player, threshold, probability bar, current stat progress in the game, and movement since game started. Two-column grid maximizes density.

Level 4 -- Season Context

  • Playoff Path: Exact same ProgressionLadder cards from MyStuff. Both teams side by side.
  • Win Total: Gauge showing all thresholds (20+ through 65+) with the "interesting" one (nearest 50%) highlighted. Current wins marker.
  • Finals Matchup Heat Map: Instead of a list, a color-coded grid where intensity = probability. Shows 10 matchups in the space of 3 rows.
  • Awards: Compact table. Players grouped, sorted by probability. Source dots show cross-platform verification.
  • Trade Watch: Simple list, pink accent for visual distinction.
  • Stat Leaders: Chip layout for low-probability items (mostly 0.1-3%).

Cross-source merge examples

  • Odds APIKalshiPolymarket NBA Champion: 13.5%, 13.5%, 11.5% -> merged to 13.5%
  • KalshiPolymarket Make Playoffs: 99.5%, 100% -> merged to 100%
  • KalshiPolymarket MIP (NAW): 47.5%, 43.5% -> merged to 45.5%

What's NOT shown (filtered out)

  • MLS "Eastern Conference Champion: Atlanta" (wrong sport -- soccer not basketball)
  • Kon Knueppel 207th three pointer novelty (irrelevant)
  • Win total thresholds at 99.5% (effectively resolved -- collapsed into gauge)
