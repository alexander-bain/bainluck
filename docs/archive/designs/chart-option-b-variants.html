Interactive Highlight — 4 Variants

All use the same interaction model (muted field + highlighted teams) but differ in visual treatment, legend placement, and information density. Real MLB World Series data.

Variant 1 Recommended

Ethereal

Smooth bezier curves, soft glow on highlighted lines, ghosted field. Minimal axis treatment — only what's needed. End-of-line labels with current probability. Feels premium and quiet.

Variant 3 Best for Golf

Field + Focus

Non-highlighted teams collapse into a single translucent band showing the range of "the field." Highlighted teams stand out against this context. Perfect for golf where 40+ players are bunched between 1–8%.

World Series 2026 Shaded area = range of non-highlighted teams
Best for Golf and other contexts with many participants. The "field" band answers "where does my player sit relative to everyone else?" without spaghetti. Also great for NFL/NCAA with 30+ teams.
Variant 4

Card Tips

Each highlighted team gets a floating stat card pinned to the end of its line — current probability, 7-day delta, and a mini trend word. Type to search and add teams. Most information-dense variant.
