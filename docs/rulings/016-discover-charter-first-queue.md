# RULING 016 — Discover charter addendum: the first queue is one arc

date: 2026-08-10
author: Alex
via: Fable, ratified
issues: #1544

**DO NOT REMOVE (CI-guarded).**

> The first Discover queue is the **multi-angle story card + cohort-compare as ONE arc.**
> Cohort-compare is **the comparison surface inside the story card**, not a separate feature.
>
> **Entry ticket unchanged: no interestingness claim without the temporal holdout.**

## Why one arc and not two queues

Split into two, cohort-compare becomes a standalone comparison surface — and this product has a
standing ruling against exactly that. *The blend is the product:* one number per question, with
comparison surfaces as the rare, deliberate exception rather than the default. A free-standing
compare view is the Kalshi-shaped thing the positioning exists to refuse.

Inside a story card it is a different object. The story is the unit; the cohorts are **how that
one story is told from more than one angle**. Same pixels, opposite meaning — which is why the
sequencing is the ruling rather than a scheduling preference. Build the compare surface first and
it has nowhere to live but its own page, and the shape hardens before the story arrives.

It also follows the standing *story is the ranking unit* rule down one level: if a story is what
gets ranked, a story is what gets rendered, and the angles belong to it.

## The entry ticket is unchanged, and it is a real gate

**No interestingness claim without the temporal holdout.** Not softened by this ruling, not
implied to be satisfied by it.

The reason it stays hard: the interestingness blend has been live at 20% weight against weights
that were **never calibrated on labelled data** — a fact the codebase itself records. A story card
is a surface built to carry an interestingness judgment, so shipping one without the holdout would
be the first time the uncalibrated blend got a purpose-built stage. The card can ship on
deterministic signals; **a claim that a story is interesting cannot ship on an unvalidated model.**

Note the pattern this shares with ruling 014's four-cycle debt: a measurement obligation that
never wins a ranking against the feature it is supposed to gate. Naming the holdout as an *entry
ticket* rather than an acceptance criterion is what stops it being deferred one more cycle — it is
paid before, not after.
