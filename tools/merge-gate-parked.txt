# merge-gate-parked.txt — shas that are GRANTED, NOT on master, and deliberately
# not being offered. Read by `tools/merge-gate.sh --orphans` (notice 31b).
#
# WHY THIS FILE EXISTS
#
# The orphan sweep asks one question — "a token was granted; why is this not on
# master?" — and there is a legitimate answer that is neither "merged" nor
# "somebody forgot": it is being held on purpose. Without somewhere to say so,
# every held sha is reported as an orphan on every run, and a report that is
# mostly known noise stops being read. That is the failure mode the sweep was
# built to cure, arriving by the back door.
#
# A PARK IS NOT A DROP. Parked shas are counted and printed in their own section
# on every run, with the reason below. Parking something removes it from the
# ORPHANS list and from nowhere else.
#
# FORMAT — one per line, and the reason is the rest of the line:
#
#   <sha>  <reason, including who is holding it and since when>
#
# The sha may be short (>=7 hex) or full; it matches a candidate when it is a
# prefix of that candidate's full sha. The reason is not optional: a line with a
# sha and nothing after it is reported as a WARN by the sweep, because a park
# nobody explained is how work goes quiet for eight sessions.
#
# WHEN A PARK ENDS, DELETE THE LINE. The sweep classifies merged shas before it
# ever looks here, so a line whose sha has landed is invisible rather than wrong
# — which is worse than being wrong. Prune on sight.
