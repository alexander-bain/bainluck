⛔ WITHDRAWN AS G1 EVIDENCE BY CAL-P098 — 2026-08-25. The measurements below are
   real and are kept verbatim; the CLAIM they were filed under is withdrawn.

   `C-FOLD-REWRITE-1` graded this artifact and returned BLOCK on three grounds,
   all of them correct:

   1. It says "same session". It was not. Each side is a separate HTTP POST to
      `/api/admin/db-query`, each POST gets its own `get_db` session, and no
      REPEATABLE READ transaction spans the pair. On a continuously-ingesting
      population, two matching digests taken from two snapshots mean nobody
      wrote in between — a fact about the clock, not about the SQL.
   2. An ordered MD5 is not `EXCEPT ALL`. It cannot say WHICH rows differ, how
      many, or whether duplicate cardinality by `outcome_id` moved.
   3. MOD 997 / 9973 is not the frozen sample. G1 pins MOD 64 and MOD 257, at
      least eight non-adjacent residues, residue 0 and both edges. The rail
      could not reach that sample — its row path is fixed at 10 s — so an
      instrument ceiling was reported as a sampling choice.

   What these 11 samples DO establish: over 1,398 published rows, no divergence
   was found. That is useful and it is not the gate.

   The replacement is `scripts/verify_fold_narrowing_row_identity.py`, rewritten
   DB-direct: one REPEATABLE READ READ ONLY transaction, bilateral `EXCEPT ALL`,
   duplicate cardinality, buckets demoted to a secondary line, and the frozen
   residue plan. Its evidence lands at
   `GET /api/admin/fold-narrowing-gate/last`.
